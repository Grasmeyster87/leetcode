61. Rotate List

Medium
<p>Given the <code>head</code> of a linked&nbsp;list, rotate the list to the right by <code>k</code> places.</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong class="example">Example 1:</strong></p>
<img alt="" style="width: 450px; height: 191px;" src="https://assets.leetcode.com/uploads/2020/11/13/rotate1.jpg">
<pre><strong>Input:</strong> head = [1,2,3,4,5], k = 2
<strong>Output:</strong> [4,5,1,2,3]
</pre>

<p><strong class="example">Example 2:</strong></p>
<img alt="" style="width: 305px; height: 350px;" src="https://assets.leetcode.com/uploads/2020/11/13/roate2.jpg">
<pre><strong>Input:</strong> head = [0,1,2], k = 4
<strong>Output:</strong> [2,0,1]
</pre>

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Constraints:</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>The number of nodes in the list is in the range <code>[0, 500]</code>.</li>
	<li><code>-100 &lt;= Node.val &lt;= 100</code></li>
	<li><code>0 &lt;= k &lt;= 2 * 10<sup>9</sup></code></li>
</ul>

61. Поворот списка

<p></p>
<p><strong class="example">Пример 1:</strong></p>
<img alt="" style="width: 450px; height: 191px;" src="https://assets.leetcode.com/uploads/2020/11/13/rotate1.jpg">
<pre><strong>Ввод:</strong> head = [1,2,3,4,5], k = 2
<strong>Вывод:</strong> [4,5,1,2,3]
</pre>

<p><strong class="example">Пример 2:</strong></p>
<img alt="" style="width: 305px; height: 350px;" src="https://assets.leetcode.com/uploads/2020/11/13/roate2.jpg">

<strong>Выходные данные:</strong> [2,0,1]
